Install guide

Three steps.

Doomloader is installed from a file, not from the Play Store, so Android will ask you to allow it once.

Download

Tap the download button on the Doomloader card once it is live.

Allow install

When Android asks, allow installs from your browser. Android may show a warning for apps outside the Play Store. That is normal.

Open and use

Open the file, tap Install, then launch Doomloader.

Only download Doomloader from this page. Use it only for content you have the right to save.
